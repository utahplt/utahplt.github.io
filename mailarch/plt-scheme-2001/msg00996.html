>  * X: 'no-resize-border works for frame% with many window managers.

Eliminating the border completely for 'no-resize-border was too much. I
committed and exp-tagged a change to Frame.cc so that the border is
eliminated only when 'no-resize-border and 'no-caption are both
specified.

individually, but I don't have a window manager handy to test it.
