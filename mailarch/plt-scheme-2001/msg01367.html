Mark, I can't speak for Shriram (who actually knows a lot about guile
and Scheme & language design), but I say:

Free Software is great.   The PLT Scheme suite is Free Software too.
That's not the issue.

The GNU project was an effort to build a Free version of Unix.  Unix
seems to be the best OS, but it's not that great, and you don't need
sky-high design standards to build a Unix.  So the GNU project has
never had high design standards.

Guile is no worse than Emacs Lisp, and folks here like Emacs fine.
The line seems to be: Emacs is a fine editor, even though Emacs Lisp
is a dog, but that's OK for a text editor.  

I extrapolate: you don't need high design standards in GNU.  But the
PLT folks are very serious about high design standards in their Scheme
work.  That can creates tension, no?
