> Apologies for my ignorance, but doesn't the patenting of parts of .net

I'm not sure what patents you're referring to.  I'm sure there are and
will be lots of patents related to .NET, but the spec itself is supposed
to be open and standardized (your views on ECMA may influence how you

doesn't prevent anyone from creating their own JVMs, and indeed several
