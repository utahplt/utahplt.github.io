Binaries for the current CVS version of PLT Scheme are available at

  http://download.plt-scheme.org/binaries/pre-release/

Currently, binaries are available for Windows, Red Hat Linux, Solaris,
and Mac OS X.

These binaries are generated automatically each night directly from the
CVS archives.

These binaries should be especially useful to Windows and Mac users, who
may not have their own compilers available.
