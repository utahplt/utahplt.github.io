Hi all,

We are pleased to announce the second release, v. 1.1, of the /surfies/
being ported to PLT Scheme.  The SRFIs included are:

   1, 2, 6+, 8, 9, 13, 14, and 19

+ this one is supported by the core of PLT Scheme.  We count it here,
so that you know you can use it.

You can get the .plt packaged version at:

http://prdownloads.sourceforge.net/schematics/srfi-1.1.plt

or you can follow the development version via CVS:

cvs -d:pserver:anonymous@cvs.schematics.sourceforge.net:/cvsroot/schematics login

cvs -z3 -d:pserver:anonymous@cvs.schematics.sourceforge.net:/cvsroot/schematics co srfi

As always, if you have any comments, or want to suggest/submit a new
SRFI you ported, or want to see ported next, please get in touch with
us, or visit http://schematics.sourceforge.net.

enjoy!
--Francisco
