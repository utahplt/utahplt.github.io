If you'd like to send mail to plt-scheme from an address other than the
one where you receive plt-scheme postings, then you can now subscribe
to

   plt-scheme-sender

at majordomo@flux.cs.utah.edu.

No postings go to subscribers of plt-scheme-sender, but subscribers of
plt-scheme-sender are allowed to post to plt-scheme.
