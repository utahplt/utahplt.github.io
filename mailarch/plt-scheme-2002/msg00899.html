The easiest way is to convert the code into a MzScheme `module' and use
the `require' form.

Example:

 file1.ss

 (module file1 mzscheme
   (provide foo)
   (define foo ...))

 file2.ss

  (module file2 mzscheme
    (require "file1.ss")
    (foo ...))
