MzScheme and DrScheme RPM's for PowerPC Linux are 
available now.

MzScheme v.101 is available from

  http://www.cs.rice.edu/CS/PLT/packages/mzscheme/

DrScheme v.101 is available from

  http://www.cs.rice.edu/CS/PLT/packages/drscheme/

After clicking the download link on either of these pages, 
choose "PowerPC Linux 2.0, RPM format" from the dropdown
list.  The files are definitely at the Rice site; it may take 
a couple of days to propagate them to mirrors.

Thanks to Paul Graunke and Robby Findler for building 
the RPM files.
