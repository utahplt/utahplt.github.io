Quoting Mr KING:
> Is it possible to draw a bitmap with a transparent color or 
> using a mask ?

MrEd can only draw monochrome bitmaps in transparent mode.

(The clipping region always affects bitmap drawing, but there's no
built-in way to construct a clipping region based on the content of a
color bitmap.)
